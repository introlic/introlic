## ⚡ What is Introlic?

**Introlic** ([introlic.in](https://introlic.in)) is an independent technology company and software venture studio founded by **Shah**. 

We engineer and ship sovereign digital platforms, consumer applications, and developer infrastructure. Introlic operates as a self-funded venture studio—designing, building, and deploying real-world software products from first principles.

Here on GitHub, we share our open-source tools, developer utilities, AI-integrated workflows, and infrastructure packages to empower engineers and the wider tech community.

## 🚀 Flagship Ventures & Products

- **[XMeeta](https://introlic.in/startups/xmeeta)** — Real-time peer discovery and low-latency interaction platform engineered to bring back genuine conversations through live video and audio.
- **[OneXads](https://introlic.in/startups)** — Sovereign ad-tech protocol, programmatic RTB exchange, and direct publisher yield monetization.
